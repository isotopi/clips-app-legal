# Terms of Service

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This app is a personal tool run by GitHub user [isotopi](https://github.com/isotopi) for their own use. It turns
highlights of gameplay streams into vertical clips and sends them, through a self-hosted copy of the open-source
scheduler [Postiz](https://github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app), as drafts to TikTok accounts owned by the operator.

1. **Who can use it.** Only the operator. There is no sign-up and no other users; the scheduler runs on the
   operator's own computer and is not reachable from the internet.
2. **What it does with TikTok.** After the operator logs in with TikTok, the app uploads the operator's own videos
   to the operator's TikTok inbox as drafts, and reads basic profile information and the operator's own videos to
   show them in the scheduler. Every post is reviewed, edited and published by the operator in the TikTok app.
3. **TikTok's terms.** Use of TikTok through this app is also subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service and
   Community Guidelines.
4. **No warranty.** The app is provided as is, for personal use, without any warranty.
5. **Changes.** These terms may change; the date above shows the latest version.
6. **Contact.** Open an issue on [this repository](https://github.com/isotopi/clips-app-legal/issues).

See also the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).
