# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This app is a personal tool run by GitHub user [isotopi](https://github.com/isotopi) for their own use (see the
[Terms of Service](terms.html)). It has one user, the operator, and no one else's data passes through it.

1. **What it stores.** The access and refresh tokens TikTok issues when the operator connects their own account,
   and the basic profile information TikTok returns for that account (display name, avatar, follower count). They
   are kept in a database on the operator's own computer.
2. **What it uses them for.** Only to upload the operator's own videos to the operator's TikTok inbox as drafts,
   and to show the connected account and its own videos in the scheduler.
3. **Sharing.** Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. Videos and captions go only to TikTok, at the operator's
   request.
4. **Other people.** The app does not collect, store or process data about any other TikTok user.
5. **Stopping.** The operator can disconnect the account in the scheduler, and can revoke the app's access at any
   time in TikTok's settings, where the apps connected to the account are managed.
6. **Contact.** Open an issue on [this repository](https://github.com/isotopi/clips-app-legal/issues).
